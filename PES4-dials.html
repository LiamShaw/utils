Atlas LBO - Session 4 - the four dials, per point

Measured in the "S4 Revenue" tab of the Atlas LBO model. Plan position: price growth 2.0 percent, volume growth 3.5 percent, churn 1.5 percent, mix and cross-sell 0.5 percent. Values in EUR millions.

The answer, per point

One point up on mix and cross-sell moves the IRR the most in this model: +2.14pp, against +2.11pp for price growth and volume growth, and -2.18pp for one point more churn (one point less churn is worth +2.11pp, the same as one point of price). Price, volume and churn are mathematically the same lever here; mix leads only because it starts from the smallest base (0.5 percent). The model gives every extra euro of revenue the same incremental margin, about 25 percent, so it cannot rank price above volume. That ranking lives in the case economics: about 0.8m of EBITDA per point of price, against 0.15 to 0.25m per point of volume.

Price and volume draw the exact same line: in this model they are the same lever. Churn mirrors price: one point more churn equals one point less price. Mix keeps the same shape, a hair steeper per point.

Method: every move was made in a scratch copy of "Atlas_LBO Model_Session 4.xlsx" and recalculated by Excel itself; 49 scenarios were recorded (the base, each dial at plus and minus one and two points, random combinations, and the three option translations using the workbook's one-off shock block). The sliders on this page run the same formulas. They were checked against every Excel run: 1029 compared values across 49 scenarios, largest deviation 1.06e-11. Run on 16 October 2026.

Base case (the file as shipped): IRR 27.81 percent, MOIC 3.41x, sponsor MOIC 3.02x, year-5 EBITDA 19.38m. The original workbook was never modified.
