DUSTER Methodology Overview

DUSTER is a tool developed to help teams improve story point estimations based on CURSE by Bill DeVoe. By splitting complexity into Development and Testing facets, teams uncover realistic hidden work.

  • D - Development Complexity: How intricate or involved the coding work is.
  • U - Uncertainty: Certainty or lack thereof regarding tech or architecture.
  • S - Scope: Breadth and reach of requested changes.
  • T - Test Complexity: Workload and integration complexity for verification.
  • E - Effort: Execution time typically associated with points.
  • R - Risk: Potential production or architectural danger.
Real-World Scenario Examples

Example 1: Dropping a DB Column
Low complexity, scope, and effort, but High Risk (could break production). Results in higher story points.

Example 2: Manual Data Migration
Moving 5,000 records manually has low complexity and uncertainty, but High Effort and Risk. Results in a large point estimate.
