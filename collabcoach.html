The Craft Collaborator

Your AI Thinking Partner

It is easy to get caught up in the daily rush. Sometimes we move so fast that we forget why we are doing the work—whether that is improving our own habits, protecting our team's well-being, or sticking to our company values. Why not ask your AI tools to act as a thoughtful collaborator? It can help you see your own work through the eyes of what you truly aim to achieve.

Pick a starter example, or start from scratch:
Inclusive Language

Hold the line on psychological safety, honesty, and trust over top-down control.

How do you like to be questioned?

Pick the collaboration voice that helps you pause and reflect without making you defensive:
The Friendly Comedian

Uses gentle humor and absurdity. Points out funny corporate buzzwords: "What's the deal with this email? Are we working or just looking busy?"

The Curious Kid

Asks simple, honest "Why?" questions over and over until you peel away the corporate rationalizations and find the real truth.

The Calm Mentor

Asks quiet questions to help you see the human situation[cite: 3], step back from quick reactions, and look at the big picture[cite: 5, 6].

The Practical Realist

Checks team capacity, hours in the day, and realistic energy. Asks who actually has to carry the heavy load on Monday morning.

Set the ground rules

A Real Reflective Pause

The AI reviews your draft, asks 2 thoughtful questions about your goals, and then offers an honest rewrite[cite: 6].

A Quick Check

Prints a quick 2-bullet gut check right above the rewritten message to save time.

No fake praise Ban openers like "Great job!" or "Certainly!" Jump straight into honest, caring feedback.
Cut corporate buzzwords Replace words like "synergy," "bandwidth," and "touch base" with clear, human language.
The "Do Nothing" check (Option Zero) Remind me what happens if I don't send this email or cancel the meeting entirely.
