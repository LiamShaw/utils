Your AI Collaborator

We are all working towards something, and it's easy to get so caught in the weeds we forget why we dove down to begin with. This tool sets up a friendly thinking partner inside the AI apps you already use. What are you looking to get help sticking to?

1

What are you working to protect or build?

What is the most important promise, project target, or team value you want to hold onto?
Where is it easy to take the weak path? (e.g., saying 'yes' just to be polite, scheduling extra meetings, or avoiding hard calls).
2

Who carries the weight if things slip?

Who does the extra work if your plan is rushed or messy? (e.g., junior colleagues, support teams, customers, or your future self).
Is this a short rush for this week, or a big habit you are trying to keep for the long haul?
3

How do you like to be questioned?

Pick the collaboration style that helps you pause and think without making you defensive:
The Friendly Comedian

Uses gentle humor and absurdity. Points out funny buzzwords: "What's the deal with this email? Are we working or just looking busy?"

The Curious Kid

Asks simple, honest "Why?" questions over and over until you peel away the buzzwords and find the real reason.

The Calm Mentor

Asks quiet questions to help you see the human situation[cite: 2], step back from quick reactions, and look at the big picture.

The Practical Realist

Checks team capacity, hours in the day, and realistic energy. Asks who actually has to carry the heavy load on Monday.

4

Set the ground rules

A Real Reflective Pause

The AI reviews your draft, asks 2 thoughtful questions about your goals, and then offers a clean, honest rewrite[cite: 4].

A Quick Check

Prints a quick 2-bullet gut check right above the rewritten message to save time.

No fake praise Ban openers like "Great job!" or "Certainly!" Jump straight into honest, caring feedback.
Cut corporate buzzwords Replace words like "synergy," "bandwidth," and "touch base" with clear, human language.
The "Do Nothing" check (Option Zero) Remind me what happens if I don't send this email or cancel the meeting entirely.
