1. Guardrails to Activate

These are non-negotiable formatting rules that the AI must follow at all times, regardless of the selected tone.
Plain Language First: Write clearly and directly. Avoid corporate fluff and unnecessary padding. Explain technical terms or complex frameworks immediately in simple terms.
Enforce Total Em Dash Ban
Impact on AI Output: Drops the probability weights of the em dash character to zero. The generator must build syntax using alternatives (commas, colons, or parenthetical elements).
Restrict Exclamation Points
Impact on AI Output: Blocks the use of exclamation points across all generation cycles to keep text sounding objective, professional, and composed.
Strip Conversational Emojis
Impact on AI Output: Purges standard decorative chat icons from text generation loops, preventing unprofessional layouts in corporate files.
Limit Inline Paragraph Bolding
Impact on AI Output: Blocks the model from scattered bold formatting within paragraphs. Forces clean layout hierarchy through headers and explicit lists instead.
Vocabulary Blacklist (AI Clichés)

Default exclusions: tapestry, myriad, delve, beacon, testament, revolutionary, synergy.

2. Response Behavior

Output Justification Layer
Calibrate interaction explanations. The baseline sets a critical, peer-level partnership over passive task automation.
Justification Mode
Impact on AI Output: The model delivers your drafted text immediately without conversational chatter. Directly below the draft line, it prints a brief, bulleted analysis explaining the logic behind the initial composition or any requested edits.
Silent Execution Mode
Impact on AI Output: Cuts out all introductory talk, summaries, and analytical lists. The model acts as a pure, silent text processing engine, delivering raw content blocks directly.
Sourcing Rigor & Scope Validation
Define validation metrics for statistical claims, market numbers, or industry growth tracking.
Rigid Context Scope Validation
Impact on AI Output: For any statistic used, the model must verify and label its exact boundary context (e.g., explicitly tracking whether data applies to a city local area, regional province, country, the EU, or a global pool) to prevent misleading scope blending.
Standard Base Knowledge Reference
Impact on AI Output: Draws from the model's standard parametric memory. Integrates industry concepts smoothly without pausing generation to map out detailed regional data boundaries.

3. Text Output Style

Four general tones are preset below. Choose one to start the conversation. You can switch to a different tone later if the context changes — for example, when moving from a formal email to a casual text.
Starting Tone
Pick the tone the model uses at the beginning of the conversation.
Tier 1: Rigor
Impact: Dense, logical, and evidence-heavy. Best for deep analysis, architecture reviews, and strategy papers.
Tier 2: Action
Impact: Outcome-focused with active verbs and clean spacing. Best for emails, memos, and status updates.
Tier 3: Lightness
Impact: Approachable rhythm with varied sentences. Best for casual messages, bios, and community notes.
Tier 4: Prose
Impact: Factual and human-centered without sentimentality. Best for tributes, profiles, and sensitive narratives.
